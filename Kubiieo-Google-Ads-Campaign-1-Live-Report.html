Why these matter

The competitor cloud terms are obvious. If someone is searching for Azure or GCP services, we shouldn't bid on them. Even with Phrase match, Google can occasionally drift into related territory, and these negatives are belt and braces against that.

The "wrong intent" negatives stop us paying for clicks from people who aren't buyers. Someone Googling "AWS jobs" or "AWS certification training" is not looking to hire a managed services partner. Same for "free aws tutorial". Filtering these out protects the budget for actual prospects.

03 Conversion tracking, end to end

Today I also fixed a tracking gap I found while testing the form on the /active/ landing page. The original GTM bridge for Elementor forms was relying on an older jQuery event that newer versions of Elementor Pro don't always fire. Form submissions were silently failing to fire the conversion event.

I replaced the bridge with a more robust version that listens three different ways (jQuery delegated event, native DOM event, and a DOM observer that watches for the success message appearing). Then I tested it. The conversion event fires correctly with all the form data attached: form name, form ID, form location, plus user email and phone.

Two conversion paths now active
PathHow it flowsLatency
Path A
GA4 import		 Form submit → GA4 generate_lead event → imported to Google Ads as close_convert_lead and qualify_lead 3 to 24 hours
Path B
Direct Ads tag		 Form submit → Google Ads conversion tag fires → recorded as Consultation Request - Form Submit Real time (minutes)
Why two paths

Path A feeds Smart Bidding optimisation (the "Conversions" column in Google Ads). It uses the GA4 imported events as the official signal.

Path B sits in a different goal category, so it does not double-count for bidding. Its job is faster reporting visibility. When a form is submitted, you'll see the conversion appear in Google Ads within minutes via Path B, instead of waiting overnight for the GA4 import to catch up.

Both paths still respect Google Ads attribution rules. Conversions from organic, direct, LinkedIn or any non-ad traffic source will appear in the "All conversions" column for reporting, but will not be attributed to Campaign #1 for bid optimisation. Only ad-click-attributed conversions influence Smart Bidding.

Caveat about close_convert_lead and qualify_lead

Those two GA4 events are sales funnel stages, not form submissions. They fire when a lead becomes qualified or closes as a customer. Right now, no integration is firing them automatically. They'll only count once Zoho is wired to push events back into GA4 when sales updates lead status, or until someone fires them manually. That's a follow-up worth raising with Eugene.

For the launch, the practical conversion signal is Path B (Consultation Request - Form Submit), which fires immediately on form submission. That's what you'll see populate first.
