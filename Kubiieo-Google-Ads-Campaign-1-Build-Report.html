Why this configuration

AWS-only focus: Per Baz on the kickoff call: Kubiieo is an AWS Advanced Tier Partner but doesn't yet have equivalent partner status with Azure or GCP. Spending paid budget on terms where you can't compete on partner credibility is wasted — so Campaign 1 is laser-focused on AWS only. Azure/GCP can be added in Phase 2 once an MSP relationship is established with those platforms.

Conservative budget: Baz's call: "let's start the conservative... I want to make sure that the whole end-to-end flow is working." A$50/day validates the funnel (ad → /active/ → form → Zoho) without burning budget on premium auctions. Once the first converted lead lands in Zoho, raise to A$100-150/day as the strategy doc recommends.

$5 max CPC cap: Strategy doc estimated $12-22 CPC on premium AWS keywords. The $5 cap deliberately keeps us out of those higher-tier auctions for now — we get more clicks per dollar on the entry-point and consultation-tier keywords ($6-14 range). This is fine for week 1 validation. Raise to $10-15 once we know the flow works.

Search-only, no Display, no Search Partners: Display Network on a Search campaign leaks budget to low-intent display surfaces. Search Partners include unverified sites with much lower quality. For a B2B AWS campaign in AU, both are budget burn. Off.
