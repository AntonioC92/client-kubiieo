Reading the trajectory

The first month, before structural fixes had taken hold, was paying around A$8.69 per click at a 2.03% CTR. 

Two weeks of optimisation later, CPC was already down to A$2.88 and CTR more than tripled. 

This week the campaign reached A$1.99 CPC and 9.06% CTR.

04

What's Driven the Improvement

Round 1, early May Foundation

I added Six sitelinks added, structured snippets, seven callouts, four additional RSA headlines that pushed Ad Strength from Average to Good. I also added a Negative-keyword starter list. After raising bidding ceiling from A$5 to A$15, Score climbed from 74.5% to 81%.

Round 2, week of 12 May Measurement fix

Investigation uncovered that the Consent Mode tag in GTM was defaulting every advertising and analytics consent type to denied, I fixed that and increased max bidding per click to A$18. Six more negatives added, taking the list to 29 terms. Score climbed to 84.9%.

Round 3, week of 21 May into 2 Jun Verification and efficiency

A live test confirmed the conversion tag now fires with consent granted (gcs=G111), which cleared Google's +9.8% conversion-tracking recommendation and drove most of the score jump from 84.9% to 94.6%. 

I added Google search partners for additional reach (no Quality Score impact). Eleven duplicate exact-match keywords consolidated into their phrase-match equivalents, with no loss of coverage. The score then reached 97.8%. 

05

The Open Item, Conversions

The one number that has not moved is counted conversions. After 387 ad clicks and A$1,608.57 in spend, the campaign reports zero. The Round 3 verification proved the tag fires correctly with consent granted in a direct-visit test, so the pipeline itself is working. 

I fixed all crm issues for leads to be captured properly in zoho
Near-term priorities
  • →Confirm whether this week's A$1.99 CPC holds as the system continues to learn. 
  • →Land the first counted conversion in Google Ads reporting. Daily check is monitoring.
  • →Segment search-partner performance by network in two to three weeks. Keep it on if partner clicks contribute, switch off if they only add spend.
  • →Continue the weekly search-terms review and add negatives where waste appears.
  • →Reach around 30 conversions, then migrate the bid strategy from Maximize Clicks to Maximize Conversions or Target CPA. This is the change that pulls cost-per-lead down the most.
07

In Summary

Across 47 days live, Campaign #1 has accumulated 387 clicks, 8,760 impressions, and A$1,608.57 in spend. 

The optimisation score has moved from 74.5% to 97.8%. Per-click cost has dropped from A$8.69 in the first month to A$1.99 this week, a reduction of just over 77%. CTR has climbed from 2.03% to 9.06%. 

The structural foundations are in place and the auction is responding.
