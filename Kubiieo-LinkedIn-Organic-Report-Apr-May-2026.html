What the Numbers Are Telling Us

Four signals in the data worth pulling out before reading the post table.

The audience is real and on-ICP
Signal 1

53 new followers landed in May alone, up 341.7% on April. The mix isn't random clicks: it's Principal DevOps Engineers, Lead AWS Consultants, Engineering Leaders ex-AWS, AI & Product Leaders in insurance, all exactly the buyer profile in the Google Ads ICP.

Sydney and Melbourne dominate
Signal 2

143 of 221 followers are in Greater Sydney (53.8%) or Greater Melbourne (10.9%). The geographic concentration aligns with the Google Ads targeting and means the same audience is being warmed in two places. 64.7% Australia, 35.3% international (mostly tech hubs: Bengaluru, London, Paris, Chennai).

Cadence matters more than length
Signal 3

The impressions chart shows a near-flat baseline in March, then a clear inflection point in mid-April when weekly posting started. The follower curve and the impressions curve are tracking together, which is the signal we want. Follower growth is up 7,200% versus the previous 90-day window when no posting was happening.

Leadership voices drive engagement
Signal 4

Posts published under leadership names outperform the page average. The Advanced Tier milestone (504 impressions) and the AWS Summit coffee post (150 impressions from a 50-word text post) prove personal voice still beats company voice on LinkedIn. Three of the top six posts overall come from leadership-authored content.

05

What's Working

Three patterns. Do more of these.

Pattern 1, News + a take wins

Best format on the page: AWS announcement, the problem it solves, what to do about it.

Top three posts by engagement all use it. Cost Explorer: 646 impressions. OpenSearch: 7.8%. Redshift RG: 9.43%.

Pattern 2, Leadership voices outperform

Posts published under leadership names beat the page average.

Advanced Tier milestone: 504 impressions, 7.54% engagement. AWS Summit coffee post (50 words of text): 150 impressions. Three of the top six posts overall are leadership-authored.

Keep mixing them in. Reserve founder-authored posts for company moments.

Pattern 3, The right people are following

New followers = the Google Ads ICP. Principal DevOps Engineers, Lead AWS Consultants, AI Product Leaders, Engineering Directors.

This audience is now warm for the same offers running on Google Ads. Same buyer pool, two channels.

06

What I'd Do Next

The posting rhythm is working and the right people are following. Now we turn views into conversations. Ideas for the next 60 days:

  • Send people somewhere. Most posts end with "worth a look" but don't link anywhere. Add a link to the matching landing page (cost, migration, or security) so readers have a clear next step.
  • Keep posting AWS news with a take. Pick a fresh AWS announcement, say what problem it solves, say who should care. That's the format winning right now.
  • Bring more leadership voices in. Posts under a leadership name do better than the page average. Add one each month, plus a founder post for big company moments.
  • Put a small budget behind the best post. A$50 to A$100 of Boost on the top post each month gets it in front of more of the same audience. Better than spreading small amounts everywhere.
  • Talk to the new followers. 53 of the right kind of people followed in May. Reach out directly through Sales Navigator. They've already shown interest.
  • Aim for 350 followers by end of Q3. Current pace gets us close. Inviting connections and boosting one post a month would push us over.
  • Track form submissions from LinkedIn. Right now we can't see who comes from a post to a landing page and submits a form. Setting up the tracking closes that gap.
