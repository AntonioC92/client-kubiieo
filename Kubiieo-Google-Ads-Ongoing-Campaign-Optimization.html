What this score actually measures

Google's optimization score is the platform's own assessment of how well the account is set up to perform. Anything above 80% is considered strong for an early-stage Search campaign. Reaching 81% inside the first optimization round sets a healthy baseline for everything that follows.

What This Means for Performance

The changes shift the campaign from a validation footing into a competitive one. Four specific effects follow:

  • ▸ The campaign can win in the auction. The refined bid ceiling lets the ad appear in front of buyers when they search, instead of only on the rare cheap-auction. Delivery and clicks should rise materially in the days following.
  • ▸ The ad takes up more space on the results page. Sitelinks, structured snippet, and callouts together turn the ad from one line of text into a rich block showing assessments, AWS partner tier, services, and trust signals.
  • ▸ Smart Bidding has clean data to learn from. The verified conversion stack is the difference between optimizing for clicks and optimizing for actual leads. The cost-per-lead curve starts bending downward once Google has data to weight bids around what converts.
  • ▸ Higher quality means lower CPC. Ad Strength at Good lifts Google's quality multiplier on the ad. The same auction position is now won at a lower price, even before any bid strategy change.

Consent Mode posture corrected for the Australian market Hidden blocker, now fixed

Investigation into why test conversions were not appearing in Google Ads exposed that the Consent Mode v2 tag inside GTM defaulted every advertising and analytics consent type to denied. With no cookie banner in place to update consent on user interaction, every form submission was firing the conversion tag correctly, and Google Ads was treating every ping as denied data, sending it to the modeling-only bucket rather than counting it.

Defaults changed to granted for the four ad and analytics consent types. The ads_data_redaction flag was switched off. Australia does not have a GDPR-style prior-consent requirement for analytics or advertising cookies, so this aligns Kubiieo's tracking posture with the actual legal regime the business operates under.

GTM container republished as Version 13. The Submit Lead Form goal moves from Misconfigured to Recording the moment Google's pipeline processes the next form submission (typically 3 to 15 minutes, up to a few hours for the first conversion against a brand-new action).

Bid ceiling raised to A$18 More auctions captured

Round 1's A$15 ceiling moved the campaign from Bid setting limited into Eligible status. After two weeks of live data, top-performing keywords were still showing roughly 80% Lost Impression Share due to rank, meaning Google was prepared to serve the ad more aggressively at a higher bid. The ceiling moved to A$18 to capture that remaining headroom.
