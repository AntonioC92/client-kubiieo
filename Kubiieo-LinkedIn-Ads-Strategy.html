The strategy at a glance

Three phases that hand off to each other, each one producing the audience the next phase relies on. Three creative angles run in parallel inside Phase 1, with the winning angle carrying into the Phase 2 lead magnets.

Phase 1 · running

Brand awareness

Build the Kubiieo audience inside the right ICP and read which creative angle the audience moves on.

$50/day across awareness, video views, and page follower campaigns in parallel.
Phase 2 · next

Lead capture

Turn the engaged audience into named leads through the strongest magnet from the Phase 1 winner.

Launches once Phase 1 clears 3,000 engaged people.

Conversion

Phase 1 kill rule

If cost per follower runs above $25 and engagement rate sits under 1.2% after $1,500 in spend, the targeting is wrong. Pause, audit, rebuild before topping up. We will not move to Phase 2 until the engaged audience clears 3,000 unique people.

05

After Phase 1: lead capture, then conversion

Phase 2 launches once Phase 1 produces the audience and creative signal it is meant to. Phase 3 follows when Zoho admin access lands and the engaged audience is big enough to retarget against.

Phase 2 · Lead capture

Primary magnet: "The 2026 AWS Cost Reduction Playbook", a 6 to 8-page PDF written from the AWS Cost Assessment landing page. Two follow-on magnets stand ready if their angle earns Phase 1: "AWS Security Posture Checklist" for the security audience, and "Sourcing Reality Check" for the onshore-vs-offshore audience (built on the artefact Baz shared on 23 May).

Lead routing while Zoho is pending: leads flow into sales@kubiieo.com through PagerDuty and into the #kubiieo-sales Slack channel, with a parallel weekly export to a holding list so nothing is lost. Once Zoho is live, the lead-gen form feeds straight in with Lead Source tagged per campaign.

Target CPL: $50-$75 inside the engaged audience from Phase 1.

Phase 3 · Conversion

  • Retargeting the engaged Phase 1 audience and the Phase 2 leads to the AWS Cost, Migration, and DevSecOps landing pages.
  • Lookalike audience built from TPG Telecom and Satori Group decision-maker profiles.
  • Conversation Ads from a named sender (defaulting to Baz) to senior cloud titles inside named target accounts.

Targets: cost per landing-page form submit $250-$400, Conversation Ads reply rate above 6%.
